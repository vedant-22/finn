## The point of view

Three sentences carry the whole product. Hold new work against them.

1. **FINN is a brief you finish.** It hands the investor the few things that changed, holds each against what management promised, and lets them go. Nothing in it is a feed.
2. **The spine is a promise ledger.** What management said on a call, marked kept, partly kept, missed, gone quiet or not due. Push and Pull both hang off it.
3. **The Network is a quiet advantage, not a contact list.** It has no screen of its own. A person appears beside the thing they can explain, one at a time, and where nobody fits, nothing is shown.

## The Network: what was decided in `notice`, and why

The brief's last word on the Network is "Show us what it notices that the investor wouldn't." Our own score for that line was 6, because "FINN names the person and stops."

**The idea: a notice is a join the investor would not do by hand.** The engine already did single joins (a contact is in a filing; a contact worked where an event happened). `notice` added four joins:

| Join | The notice | Why a person misses it |
|---|---|---|
| A person × the questions about them | **The path** ("Who was there"): an arrival's career as the filing lists it, one of your people at each station, one question each, then "In the room", then "Copy the call sheet" | You know Nitin ran a treasury. You don't recall it the morning a filing lists that employer |
| A filing × a filing | **Control seats**: two or more different people leave audit, risk, compliance, legal, secretarial or finance seats at one company within 30 days | Push judges a filing alone and rightly folds a compliance officer's exit |
| A contact × several questions | **One call, two answers**: one contact is the person to ask at two or more companies | Each question arrives on a different day under a different company |
| Scarcity × silence | **The only one**: the single person you know who has worked inside a holding, and it has been years | A contact list doesn't know who is irreplaceable |

**The rules that keep it honest.** Each was learnt from a reviewer catching a line that was false or would embarrass the investor. They are checks in `evals/run_network.py`; do not loosen one without reading why it exists in `docs/plans/notice.md`.

- **The filing gives no years, so nothing claims two people overlapped.** The five invented profiles that once dated real people's careers were deleted in `network-loop`; a real person carries only what a filing says, and checks assert no earlier job carries a year and no link through one says overlapped. Where a person has a path, the path speaks for them everywhere (the fold, the company page, the meeting answer, a direct "Who knows …?").
- **A real person carries only what a filing says**: titles, dates, the stated reason, printed as filed. An employer is matched to a known company only when the known name is the whole of what the filing wrote ("HDFC Life Insurance Company Limited" is not HDFC); otherwise the station keeps the filing's words and pins nobody.
- **A seat the filing's own profile opens with is a station** ("Founder & CEO of Samara Capital Group"), read only for a title that runs or owns a firm, at an organisation the registry knows. What that firm holds today is a question for the room; a contact who left is asked about their own time there.
- **A departure must be a departure.** A retirement, a completed tenure, a death, or a change of designation with nobody leaving is not part of a pattern.
- **Someone inside the company now is never asked a question** and is never a pattern's person. They are shown as "You know", with a caution.
- **No pronouns for anyone; names only. No word of suspicion** about a named real person (flag, suspicious, fled, purge, exodus, quietly). A pattern line holds the company, filed titles, a count and a span of days; its evidence holds names, titles, dates and filed reasons. No results date beside a departure: 8 of 15 companies filed a people change on a results day, so it is base rate, not a signal.
- **A question must be sayable on a call, to someone who may never have met the person.** A contact who was only at the same institution is asked about the institution from where they sat. A question about the person (an interim role, a predecessor) goes to "In the room". Every question is one of a small table of templates in `engine.py`, and the exact question of every path shown is asserted.
- **A question follows its subject, from the contact's own seat** (`network-loop`). A margin asks a competitor about costs, a fall in profit about one-off items, a lender's credit cost about credit costs; a pattern's person is asked what tests the pattern from where they sit. Every one is from the `ASK` table and names no time.
- **Said once, and counted honestly** (`network-loop`). Someone you know whose own filing is news is one row at that company, and an event there the same day joins it. A company's cover counts people, and its page lists every one it counts.
- **Silence is shown, not filled.** A station with nobody stays bare. A path needs two people to ask, or the card keeps "Who to ask". At most four pins; a contact once.
- **Rules are not tuned towards a name.** One call picks Meera Krishnan because the rule does; Nitin Agarwal also qualifies and still stands at both of his places. When a rule doesn't fire as a plan's copy says, the copy is wrong, not the data.
- **Firing counts over all 18 companies are asserted**, not only the demo case: control seats at one company, one call raised once, the only one once, five paths, a filed seat elsewhere for one person of 42.

**Rejected, and why** (so nobody builds them by reflex):

| Idea | Why not |
|---|---|
| A network map | A hairball; it shows structure, not what to do; it needs a screen of its own |
| A calendar card ("Thursday: Home First") | Needs an invented calendar. "Before a meeting" is asked, not known. Named as next |
| Drafting the message to the contact | Needs a model reading private contact data, which ADR 1 forbids. "Copy the call sheet" instead |
| "Same house" (two arrivals from one competitor) | Obvious to its reader, and wrong in detail on HDFC Bank |
| Second-order events across a supply link | Relevance needs judgment that rules can't make |
| Themes read from a filing's words | Measured: "input tax credit" read as lending. Too noisy |
| A results date as evidence beside departures | Base rate dressed as a signal |
| Any dated overlap between a filing person and a contact | Rests on invented years |
| A model writing the questions | It would write better ones, and would need the contact list in a prompt |

## Traps that have cost sessions

- **Ports 3001 and 4173 may be the user's own servers**, started from their Terminal; stopping them is refused. Run your own beside them: `FINN_API_PORT=3101 python3 pipeline/dev_api.py` and `FINN_API_PORT=3101 npx vite preview --port 4273`, then e2e on `http://localhost:4273`.
- **A local API left running is stale.** `pipeline/dev_api.py` loads the engine once. Restart it after an engine change or `flows.py` tests old code.
- **After a deploy, wait for the new asset hash** (`curl -s https://finn-network.vercel.app | grep -o 'assets/index-[A-Za-z0-9_-]*\.js'` against `web/dist/assets`) before any e2e on the link. A deploy that prints "fetch failed" can still show HTTP 200 on the old build.
- **`vite preview --port N` moves to the next free port without saying so**, and keeps the API port it was started with. On 15 Nov a preview and API left up since 02:20 meant a later run tested an API that had loaded the engine before it changed. After an engine change start a fresh API and preview on ports nothing holds, with `--strictPort` (`lsof -iTCP:<port> -sTCP:LISTEN` first).
- **A refused deploy is not retried** and never holds `STAGE`. It goes in `docs/NEEDS_FROM_USER.md`.
- **`heartbeat:` commits are the runner's.** Leave `docs/HEARTBEAT.md` alone.
- **Reviewers recall facts about real companies from memory.** Nothing may rest on a fact that is not in a filing in the repo.
- **A fixed wait for an `/api` answer fails on a cold start on the link.** Wait for the answer's element instead. Measured 15 Nov: a cold `/api/network` instance takes about 10 s to rebuild the views for one Same person answer. `flows.py` now waits for elements at every such step.
- **Back-to-back test runs against the link trip Vercel's bot protection.** On 15 Nov, after about ten runs of the e2e scripts in an hour, requests from this laptop were first slow-walked (a static JSON file pending for 33 s, so `flows.py` stalled at a different step each run) and then answered 403 with `x-vercel-mitigated: challenge`. The app was fine: a fetch from another address got it. So a stall at a random step on the link is not the app until one plain `curl -I` says 200; run `flows.py` on the link once per deploy, not in a loop, and leave minutes between scripts.
- **The attachments are about 31 MB once mail encodes them**, over Gmail's 25 MB, because the bundle is 21 MB: the bundle goes as a link (NEEDS step 5).
- **The public branch must pass as a cloner runs it.** `git archive submission` into an empty folder, a fresh virtualenv, then `evals/gate.py` and `evals/claims.py`, in that order too. On 15 Nov a gate run on a clone rewrote `evals/annual_result.txt` to 36/36 (a clone has no report PDFs, so one check is skipped), and `claims.py` then failed on README's 37/37. `run_annual.py` now leaves the record alone when it skips that check. A result file an eval rewrites must read the same on a clone.
- **Review loops stall near 8.5.** Three rounds at most, and stop when a round moves the average by less than 0.3.
