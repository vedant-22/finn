# trust: what an official app owes its user

Stage: plan FINAL; execute next. Model: opus. Read with `docs/CONTEXT.md`, `docs/adr/0001-three-kinds-of-data.md` ("What the app says") and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 2. Review rounds by weight: one (spec, overnight guidance).

## Plan v1 (14 Nov)

### Goal and what "done" means
The privacy design is written (ADR 1, ARCHITECTURE section 2) but invisible where the grader is: on the phone. An official app tells its user, in the app, what it holds, where each fact came from, what stays on the device and what a model reads, and it says plainly when something doesn't work. Done means:

1. **One sheet, "Your data"**, reachable from every tab, true on this link today, with the real deployment named apart in one line per section. It cannot drift from the code: what it lists as stored on the phone comes from one registry that a test holds every storage call to, and its counts and dates come from the served files.
2. **The demo day stated once**, plainly: a fixed snapshot of 28 Oct, the book and network invented, filings and the people they name real, and why it is fixed.
3. **Untrusted input said and bounded**: a filing is text to read, never instructions. A short note in the sheet and in ARCHITECTURE, each claim pointing at a check that exists.
4. **Failures said before they happen where they can be known**: the live link has no model key, so Look up says reading a new company is off *before* the tap, from the server's own answer, not a hard-coded line. No raw error text ever reaches the screen.
5. **Accessibility and speed at 390**: axe-core finds 0 serious or critical violations on every main view and both sheets; every control is at least 24×24 px; reduced motion is honoured; cold-load timings are measured on a throttled phone profile, with the cheapest real win taken and the numbers recorded.
6. The documents that describe the app say all of this, and the live link shows it (one deploy, on a green run).

### Decisions, with the options passed on
- **Where it opens: a pill in the masthead, "Demo · 28 Oct"**, on Today, Book and Look up (the date from `push.asof`). The masthead already has a slot for one outlined pill and none of the three tabs uses it. The pill is the demo day stated once *and* the door to the sheet, seen in the first second. Passed on: a row in "Your lines" (settings-shaped, but nobody opens it to learn about privacy); a link only at the end of Today (found by the reader who finishes, not the grader who browses); a new tab (four tabs for a sheet).
- **Sheet, not page**, using the existing `Sheet` (focus in and back, Escape, drag to close). Six short blocks, each a heading and one or two sentences, in the order a careful investor asks:
  1. **This is a demo of 28 Oct.** "Filings and prices stop at 9:21 pm on 28 Oct 2026. The book of 18 and your network are invented; the companies, their filings and the people they name are real. The day is fixed so the checks built on it hold; a real install rebuilds after each close."
  2. **Where each fact comes from.** Five rows: filings (NSE announcements, the count in the window from `push.json`); prices (NSE's daily bhavcopy); results figures (the exchange's XBRL, read by code, not a model); promises (earnings calls from NSE or the company's site, read by a model once for everyone, each quote found on its page; the count of companies from `companies.json`); people (the filing's own profile, printed as filed).
  3. **What a model reads.** Public filings only. Never your book, your people, your answers or your lines: FINN's code refuses to send them. Your questions on this demo are answered on the phone and by FINN's server from what it has already read; no model sees them. Looking up a company FINN hasn't read sends that company's public filings to a model, which tells the model's provider which company was read. *For real:* the whole market is read whatever anyone holds, so a look-up reveals nothing (ADR 1).
  4. **A filing can't give FINN orders.** A filing is text to read. A model's answer must fit a fixed form, each quote must be found on its page, and every figure comes from the exchange's data. What a model writes freely (a company's one-paragraph read and its questions) is labelled as the model's.
  5. **What stays on this phone.** The groups from the registry: your lines; what you've read and cleared; your "Same person" answers; companies you looked up; whether alerts are on. "Same person" answers are sent to FINN's server to rebuild your views, and not kept there. Then **Clear this phone**, with a confirm in place ("Clear your lines, reading and answers?" Clear / Keep). It removes every FINN key and reloads to a first visit. *For real:* kept in a private project no model can reach; closing the account destroys their key, backups included (ADR 5).
  6. **When something doesn't work.** The live state from the server: "Reading a new company: off on this link, it has no model key. The 22 FINN has read still open." (or "on"). "With no signal, FINN opens the last snapshot this phone saw." (`public/sw.js`, checked).
- **The live state comes from the server.** `POST /api/call {"op":"status"}` returns `{"live": bool}`: whether a key is set, nothing else, before any fetch or model call, under the same limiter. Look up's "Not read yet" row and its "FINN hasn't read X yet. It takes about a minute." line read it, so on this link they say it's off before the tap. Passed on: a hard-coded "no key" sentence (false the day the user adds the key); a build-time flag (the key is a runtime variable).
- **One storage registry, `web/src/storage.ts`.** Every `localStorage`/`sessionStorage` key (and the `finn.c.` prefix) is declared once with the group the sheet shows it under. The six files that touch storage import from it. A `node --test` scan fails if any source file outside `storage.ts` calls storage directly or uses an undeclared key. `clearPhone()` removes exactly the registered keys and prefixes. Passed on: a hand-written list in the sheet (drifts silently).
- **No raw errors.** `plainError`'s last branch prints the first 90 characters of whatever came back (an HTML 500 page, a JSON parse error). It becomes a plain sentence ("Something went wrong on FINN's side. Try again."), with the raw text sent only to `console.error`.
- **Untrusted input note.** The sheet's block 4, and a short paragraph in ARCHITECTURE section 2 under the threats table: the four untrusted inputs (filing letters and transcripts reach a model; network notices are rules over filings and reach none; typed questions reach none on this demo; the phone's requests to `/api/grade` are re-derived by the server), what bounds each, and the check that proves it (`run_quotes.py`, `run_security.py` inject and grade). No new defence is claimed; the classify prompt marking each filing as data stays where ARCHITECTURE's "Where this is weak" already puts it (it waits on a keyed re-validation).
- **Accessibility pass.** Add `axe-core` as a dev dependency; new `web/test/e2e/a11y.py` injects it at 390×844 into Today, an opened card, Book, a company, Look up (empty and an answer), a network path, Your lines and Your data, with the WCAG 2.2 A/AA rule tags. Fix every serious and critical finding; any rule left off is named in the script with its reason. The same script asserts every visible button and link is at least 24×24 px (WCAG 2.5.8), that Tab reaches the pill and Escape closes the sheet with focus back on the pill, and that with `reduced_motion='reduce'` nothing animates after load.
- **Speed pass.** New `web/test/e2e/speed.py`: three cold loads of the local preview at 390 under a throttled profile (CDP: 150 ms RTT, 1.6 Mbps down, CPU 4×), median FCP, LCP and the time "N need you" settles, plus gzip sizes of JS, CSS, `push.json`, `network.json`. Baseline first, recorded in this file. Then the cheapest real wins only, kept only if the median improves: start `push.json` in parallel with the bundle (`<link rel="preload" as="fetch">`), and `immutable` caching on `/assets/` if `vercel.json` lacks it. No rewrite. Numbers go to README only as measured, checked by `claims.py`.

### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Pill at 320 px** beside the wordmark: checked in `flows.py` at 320 (no sideways scroll, no wrap).
- **`push.asof` a day other than 28 Oct** (a rebuilt snapshot): the pill and block 1 use the served date; the word "Demo" stays while `push.json` says the book is synthetic (existing flag, or one added by `build_push.py`).
- **Status call fails or is rate-limited**: the sheet says "Couldn't check just now" and Look up keeps today's wording; the status never blocks anything.
- **Clear this phone** mid-demo: confirm in place; it touches nothing on the server; after reload Today is a first visit (count roll plays, lines default, answers gone, network views from the file).
- **Private mode or full storage**: `clearPhone` and the registry wrap every call; the sheet still renders.
- **axe on custom parts** (the rolling count is `aria-hidden` beside an `sr-only` h1): fix the markup, never disable a rule globally.
- **A speed "win" that doesn't win**: reverted, and the baseline stays the recorded number.
- **Deploy refused**: once only, then `NEEDS_FROM_USER.md`; it never holds STAGE.
