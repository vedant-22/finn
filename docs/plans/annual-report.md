### Goal and what "done" means
The brief names annual reports as a primary source (rows 15, 37, R4, R5 of `docs/BRIEF_TRACE.md`) and asks where "management's story and the actual financials agree or diverge" (row 33). FINN reads only calls today. Done means:

2. Code, not a model, reads three things from the cut pages: **the auditor** (opinion as modified or not, each emphasis-of-matter or going-concern paragraph, the audit-trail exception), **contingent liabilities** (claims not acknowledged as debt and the other Schedule III heads, both years), **related parties** (the holding company, if there is one, and its lines). Every line shown is quoted from its page and passes `verify_quote` on the cited page; every figure shown is a string on that page.
3. The company page gets one fold, **The annual report**, and the notes can add at most two questions to *Ask on the next call*, from a fixed table of templates, when a rule fires.
4. Measured on hand-read labels with a held-out half fixed now (below); the docs' "works" claim is gated on thresholds set now.
5. The note, README, ARCHITECTURE, BRIEF_TRACE, an ADR, INTERVIEW_DEFENSE, CONTEXT and DECISIONS say what was built and what was not. `claims.py` (its pending phrases rewritten), `ship.py --write`, the checklist's pass, then the task's one deploy on a green run, which also carries `checkpoint`'s review fixes to the link.

### Decisions, with the options passed on
- **Rules, not a model, read the notes.** (a) A model on the cut pages: no key tonight, so it would ship nothing, and figures read by a model need the same page check anyway. (b) Rules: the three notes are regulated forms (Schedule III's contingent-liability heads; Ind AS 24; SA 705 and 706 headings, "Our opinion is not modified in respect of this matter"; Rule 11(g) for the audit trail), so their wording repeats across companies in a way prose does not. (c) Rules now, a model for layouts the rules can't read once a key exists: designed in the ADR, not built. Chosen: (b), with (c) named. The page check makes a wrong figure impossible only if the figure is on the page; the cross-checks below make a figure from the wrong row unlikely, and the labels measure it.
- **Two stages: cut, then read.** The cutter selects pages from the whole PDF by headings (the financial-statement audit reports, the contingent-liability note, the related-party note, one page either side for breaks) and records the PDF's sha256 and page count. The reader works only on the cut pages. The fixture is the cutter's output, so CI never needs NSE, and `ask` can retrieve from the same pages.
- **Consolidated first.** The notes are read from the consolidated statements when the report has them, otherwise standalone (Home First has no consolidated set). The label says which.
- **A yardstick a PM uses.** Claims not acknowledged as debt are set against FY26 profit after tax from the company file ("₹531 crore, 1.7 years of FY26 profit"). Net worth is not in the company file and is not derived from a rounded debt-to-equity. For a bank, the Schedule 12 lines other than claims (guarantees for customers, forward contracts) are its business, not a risk to size: only claims are shown, with one sentence saying why.
- **Related parties, thin.** Only the holding company (the note's own "Holding company" entry) and its transaction lines, both years, the largest as a share of FY26 revenue (Maruti's royalty). A company with no holding company says so in one line with the note's pages. Table layouts differ (a party under each transaction, transactions under each party, a matrix), so this part is built last and cut first: if its held-out check misses, it shows the holding company's name and the pages only.
- **Base rate is not a signal.** The probe found the audit trail named in nearly every report. If more than half of the 21 carry an audit-trail exception, it is shown as a count ("as in 14 of the 21 reports read") under the auditor block, never as a highlight or a question. Key audit matters are routine and sit in two-column tables rules can't split: not read, named as next.
- **Where it shows.** A fold on the company page, between *The numbers* and *For and against*: three short blocks (The auditor, Contingent liabilities, Related parties), each a sentence made by code from parsed fields, then the verbatim lines with `url#page=N`. Its foot: "Read by FINN's rules from the FY26 annual report filed on NSE on 2 Jul 2026. Each line is quoted from its page; no model read it." Passed on: a Today card (the reports were filed months before the window and none falls in it with a finding), a Book column (a second place to say one thing), a screen of its own.
- **Questions from the notes.** One table of templates, each sayable on a call and naming no person: an emphasis of matter ("The auditor drew attention to note 59(a) of the consolidated accounts. What would settle it, and by when?"); claims of at least one year's profit ("Claims not acknowledged as debt are ₹X crore, about N years of profit. Which of them is closest to a ruling?"); claims up by a quarter or more ("Claims not acknowledged as debt rose from ₹A crore to ₹B crore. What was added this year?"). At most two a company; `whoAsked` names the annual report as a writer. Firing counts over all 22 are asserted, as the Network's are.
- **KFin's cover letter.** The cutter falls back to screener's annual-report link (the same fallback the transcripts use), fetched through `pdf.fetch` and its public-address check; if that fails, the page says the exchange copy is a cover letter and nothing is read.
- **Live look-ups get no annual block.** The API never fetches an annual report (55 MB is not a request-time job); a company outside the 22 says so in the fold's place. The pipeline alone fetches, with its own 80 MB cap; the server's 40 MB cap is unchanged.

### Edge cases and failure modes
| Case | Handling |
|---|---|
| Units (₹ crore, lakh, million, thousand) | Read from the page's "All amounts in ..." line or the table's "(₹ in crore)"; no unit found on the page means the block shows pages only. Tested conversions. |
| A row split over lines; figures on their own lines; "-" or "Nil"; brackets; "*" footnotes | Parser reads a label then its two figures; unit tests on real lines from five layouts. |
| "The above includes" sub-rows | Sub-rows are never summed into a total; the total is the head row, or the printed Total row when the note has one. A total that disagrees with its printed head fails the cross-check and the block shows pages only. |
| An emphasis of matter outside the financial-statement audit (sustainability, a subsidiary's auditor quoted) | Taken only inside "Independent Auditor's Report ... on the (Consolidated / Standalone) Financial Statements", up to "Key Audit Matters" or the next heading. Dixon's sustainability one is a negative label. |
| Standalone and consolidated both carry the paragraph | Shown once, from the set the block reads. |
| A note spanning two pages | Cut keeps one page either side; `verify_quote` checks the next page for breaks. |
| A scanned page | Counted; a note on a page with no text says it couldn't be read. |
| An annual report filed after 28 Oct | Not used: the snapshot's day holds. None today; asserted. |
| Text in a filing that reads like an instruction | Nothing executes or prompts on it; React renders it as text; no model reads it (ARCHITECTURE's untrusted-input row gains the annual report). |
| A rule fires on no company or on all | Firing counts asserted; a rule that fires on more than half is base rate and becomes a count. |
