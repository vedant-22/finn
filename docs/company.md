# ENAM, in half a page

ENAM was founded in 1984 by Vallabh Bhansali and Nemish Shah and grew into one of India's most respected investment banks before the broking and banking arm went to Axis in 2012. ENAM Holdings is what remains: a long-horizon investor with its own capital. Public material describes a bottom-up, fundamental approach looking for durable advantage and execution ability, and puts very heavy weight on the quality and integrity of management and governance. Nemish Shah is known for patient, contrarian, margin-of-safety positions held for years. There are ethical exclusions (no intoxicants, gambling, harm to living creatures).

What a day looks like, as far as I can tell: few names, deep work, lots of time listening to management, very little trading. The brief says the same: "a small team makes high-conviction decisions and lives with the outcomes."

What they would love:
- A tool that judges management by its record, quarter after quarter. That is their core skill, and it is mostly kept in people's heads.
- Primary sources, cited to the page.
- Quiet software. Fewer alerts, each one earned.

What would annoy them:
- Trading-flavoured noise: tick charts, sentiment scores, "stocks to buy".
- Summaries that repeat the press release in nicer words.
- Confident claims with no source.

Sources: 5paisa profile of Nemish Shah (https://5paisa.com/news/nemish-shah-the-man-who-took-infosys-public), FLAME governing body page, unofficed.com strategy notes. Light reading only; nothing here is from a conversation with ENAM.
