# Walkthrough script

About 2 minutes 15 seconds. Spoken lines are short. Don't read the screen; say why it matters.

## Recording setup

- iPhone, or Chrome devtools at 390×844 with device frame off. Light mode, notifications off, battery and clock hidden if you can.
- Open https://finn-ledger.vercel.app once before recording so fonts and data are cached. Then add it to the home screen and open from there; it runs full screen.
- Start fresh: clear the site's data (Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data on an iPhone, or devtools → Application → Clear site data), then open the app again. It should say "2 need you" (the 28 Oct 9:21 pm snapshot).
- Pick the live ticker in advance (ASTRAL works) and read it once off camera so the server has the transcripts cached. The recorded run then takes about 20 seconds instead of a minute. Clear site data again afterwards.
- Record the screen with a mic. Speak after each tap, not over it.

## Beats

**1. Today (0:00–0:20)**
Open the app. "2 need you", and the sentence under it names Syngene and Larsen & Toubro.
> "This is FINN. It's for someone holding fifteen or twenty stocks for years."
> "It opens on how many things need me today. Two, out of nine filings and price moves."
Tap "Week". The count rolls to 6 and the cards change. Tap "24h" to go back.
*(silence, 1 second while it rolls back)*

**2. A card (0:20–0:35)**
Point at Syngene's card: the month's line, +12.2% on 28 Oct, the CFO gone, profit down 52% on a year ago. Under the sentence: "Past your ±4% and 2× lines, 43.5× volume".
> "Each card is one event, and it says which of my lines it crossed."

**3. The full view (0:35–1:10)**
Tap "Read more". The card grows into its page.
> "Read more opens it in place. Nothing jumps."
The CFO's exit is first, with his reason in the filing's own words and its page.
> "The CFO left with the results. That's the filing's own sentence, not a summary."
Scroll to the results table.
> "The four numbers, against last quarter and last year. Straight from the exchange's data file."
Scroll to "Against their word".
> "Then the part I care about. What did they promise, and did they do it."
Tap the first Ask FINN question. The answer appears in place.
> "I can ask right here. The answer only comes from their calls and filings."
Tap "Back". The page shrinks into the same card, at the same scroll.

**4. The end and my lines (1:10–1:30)**
Scroll down. Open "Worth a look (4)" for a second, then close it. Scroll to the blue band: "That's the last 24 hours."
> "And then it ends. I know I've seen everything that matters."
Point at the next-call questions.
> "It leaves me one question for each company's next call."
Tap "Change your lines". The sheet comes up. Tap − next to "Price move in a day" twice; the count in the sheet goes up.
> "The lines are mine. Tighter lines, more alerts."
Tap "Reset to defaults", then drag the sheet down.

**5. Book and the ledger (1:30–1:50)**
Tap "Open your book". The counts across the book sit at the top: 4 missed, 17 gone quiet, 32 kept, 93 not due.
> "This is every promise across my book, and who's keeping theirs."
Tap "Look up". Type "Did L&T deliver on revenue growth?" and tap "Ask".
> "Last year they said fifteen percent. They did under twelve. This year's guide is on track so far."
Tap "Aug '25 call, page 9" under the quote; let the PDF open on that page for a second; go back.
> "Every quote is checked against the PDF by code. If it isn't on the page, FINN doesn't show it."

**6. Any company (1:50–2:10)**
In the Look up field, type ASTRAL and tap "Check their word". It says FINN hasn't read ASTRAL yet. Tap "Read ASTRAL".
> "It works on any NSE company. It's reading their last five calls right now."
Let the steps run. When the record opens:
> "Same ledger. About nine cents of model time, and nothing from news or brokers."

**7. Close (2:10–2:15)**
Tap "Today". It still says 2 need you.
> "Most days FINN should be quiet. When it isn't, there's a reason."
