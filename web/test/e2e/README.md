Browser checks at phone size (Python Playwright). Each takes the base URL as its first argument (default: finn-ledger-next, or localhost:4173 for sweep).

- `flows.py` the brief read to the end (window, one event one figure, your lines from a notification, reader, Ask in place, done, reload, bring back), Book filter, a company and Back scroll, Ask by company, by book (holdings first) and an unknown symbol, a filing kind switched off, All quiet and its wider window; prints page errors; exits 1 on a failed check
- `folds.py` every fold stays visible when opened on screen
- `sweep.py` all company pages at 320 and 390 px: no sideways scroll, no NaN/undefined
- `ship.sh "message"` test, build, commit, push, deploy to finn-ledger-next; `--live` also deploys the live link (only after the stage 4 comparison)
